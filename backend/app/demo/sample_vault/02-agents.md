# The agent organisation

G-sus is the head orchestrator. It turns an objective into a plan over the capabilities that are actually registered,
delegates the steps to agents through the central orchestrator, compares and verifies their results, reports
contradictions instead of hiding them, and stops at approval checkpoints for high-impact actions. G-sus never performs
those actions itself.

Agents include the CEO (opportunity pipeline and weekly review), Chief Intelligence (market scans and trends), the CFO
(unit economics and capital allocation), Chief Risk (compliance assessment), Product Intelligence (opportunity scores),
the Transformation Lab, Supplier Intelligence, Brand Studio, Content & Creative, Advertising, the Store Generator,
Experimentation, Self-Improvement and Performance Engineering. The Rocky Flare creative engine and its council of
personas evaluate concepts from several perspectives.

Every task produces a message with a summary, findings, evidence, confidence and an epistemic status: verified,
estimate, assumption or hypothesis. Blocked and failed tasks say why. Tools are deny-by-default: an agent can use a
tool only with a grant for that tool and operation, and never more than the person it works for.
